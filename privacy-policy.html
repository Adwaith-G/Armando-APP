Who provides this app

Armando Muscle Club is a fitness and gym companion app for workouts and self-tracking. App: Armando Muscle Club. Gym: Armando Muscle Club, Kozhikode, Kerala, India. Developer and privacy contact: Adwaith G (adwaithcontact@gmail.com).

Information stored on your device

When you choose to enter it, the app stores your name, fitness profile (including age, sex, height and weight), goals, body measurements, BMI-related inputs, diet preferences, food and water logs, workouts, training and weight records, membership details (such as name, phone number, branch and dates), reminders, notes and app preferences on your device in the app’s local storage.

These records are used locally to display your plans, calculate estimates, track progress and operate reminders. The developer does not provide user accounts, a sync service, analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs in this version, and does not receive your in-app logs or profile data through app servers.

Internet connections and outside services

Most app features work locally. Where a workout image has not been packaged in the app, the app may request an exercise illustration over HTTPS from GitHub’s raw.githubusercontent.com hosting service. The hosting provider can receive technical network information such as your IP address, browser/device request headers and the image URL, and handle it under its own policies. Your locally saved health, workout and membership entries are not placed in those image requests.

You can choose to open external webpages, phone calls, email or WhatsApp from the app. When you tap an enquiry or renewal option, you control whether to send the prepared message; the third-party app/service then handles any information you choose to send under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not send those messages automatically.

Permissions and device features

Internet is used for optional exercise images and opening external sites. Notification permission (Android 13 and newer) is requested when you enable or use a water or workout reminder. Notifications may be disabled at any time in system settings or in the app. Vibration is used for timer alerts; on-device text-to-speech may be used for optional workout voice cues. The app does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

Storage, protection, retention and deletion

App data is kept in the app’s private storage on your device. This version disables Android automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup for app data, but storage security also depends on your device security settings. The app does not provide its own additional encryption for exported backup text.

Your data stays until you remove it. To delete local records, go to Profile & settings > Erase all data, or clear app storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Deleting data is permanent. There is no online user account to delete.

You can manually create a text backup in Profile & settings and choose to copy or share it. That backup can contain personal and health details; protect it carefully. Copies you voluntarily keep outside the app are not erased by the app’s deletion button.

Health-related information

BMI, calorie, macronutrient, body fat, one-rep-max, water targets and exercise illustrations are approximate educational fitness guidance only; they are not medical tests, diagnoses or personalized treatment. Do not rely on these estimates to make medical decisions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting a program or when you have medical questions.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13. Nutrition and calorie estimates in the app are designed for adults aged 18 and older. Minors should seek guidance from a parent or guardian and a qualified healthcare professional before using fitness or diet guidance.

Updates and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app or its data practices change. The most recent version will be made available within the app and on the public policy page. For privacy questions or requests, email adwaithcontact@gmail.com or contact Armando Muscle Club in Kozhikode, Kerala, India.

Need help or have a question? Email adwaithcontact@gmail.com. Also see our Terms & Conditions.
